The problem, simply

Fitness plans often expect life to stand still.

But your time, energy, experience and equipment can change from one day to the next. A rigid plan can turn one missed workout into a reason to give up.

◷

Life gets in the way

Busy days and changing schedules make fixed workout routines difficult to follow.

◎

One plan doesn't fit everyone

People have different goals, fitness levels, preferences and equipment available.

↗

Progress can feel invisible

Without meaningful feedback and milestones, it is harder to stay motivated over time.

Sādhanā's answer: adapt the training to your life — and keep your growth moving.
