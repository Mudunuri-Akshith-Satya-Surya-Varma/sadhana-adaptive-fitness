# Sādhanā — Adaptive AI Fitness Platform

**Personalized fitness. Adaptive progression. Sustainable discipline.**

Sādhanā is an AI-powered fitness and lifestyle platform designed to help people build consistency regardless of their schedules, experience, or lifestyle constraints.

Unlike conventional fitness applications that rely on fixed routines and ideal schedules, Sādhanā aims to adapt workouts, lifestyle habits, and progression to each user's circumstances and performance. Combining AI-driven personalization with RPG-inspired gamification, the platform makes fitness a continuous journey of growth, discipline, and achievement.

## 🎯 Vision

To make structured fitness and healthy lifestyle development accessible to everyone, regardless of how predictable or demanding their daily lives may be.

Sādhanā encourages users to develop discipline through realistic, adaptive plans rather than abandoning progress when life gets in the way.

## ✨ Planned Features

* **AI-Powered Personalization:** Generate personalized fitness recommendations based on user goals, experience, available time, lifestyle, and progress.
* **Adaptive Daily Plans:** Adjust recommendations based on completed activities, missed sessions, schedule changes, and user feedback.
* **Lifestyle Integration:** Consider sleep schedules, dietary habits, activity levels, and other relevant lifestyle factors.
* **RPG-Inspired Progression:** Turn fitness milestones and healthy habits into a progression system that encourages long-term engagement.
* **Unique Fitness Classes:** Categorize users into fitness-oriented classes based on their goals, preferences, abilities, and lifestyle.
* **Progress Tracking:** Monitor workout history, milestones, and improvements over time.
* **AI Fitness Assistant:** Help users understand their plans, receive exercise guidance, and report difficulties during workouts.
* **Social Fitness (Future Scope):** Enable community participation, trainer-led courses, group challenges, and shareable fitness achievements.

## 🧠 Adaptive AI Approach

Sādhanā is designed around a continuous feedback loop:

1. **Understand** — Collect relevant information about the user's goals, experience, schedule, and lifestyle.
2. **Personalize** — Recommend appropriate workouts and achievable lifestyle changes.
3. **Track** — Record completed activities and user feedback.
4. **Adapt** — Evaluate progress and recommend suitable adjustments.
5. **Progress** — Gradually develop fitness, capability, and consistency.

AI-generated recommendations will be supported by validated exercise data and backend safety rules rather than relying entirely on unrestricted model output.

## 🎮 The Sādhanā Experience

Sādhanā aims to make personal development feel like a progression journey.

Users work toward meaningful fitness goals, complete activities, build habits, and unlock milestones. The platform emphasizes sustainable improvement while maintaining accountability and encouraging users to challenge themselves realistically.

The goal is not simply to complete workouts. It is to build a lifestyle that supports continued growth.

## 🔒 Safety and Responsible AI

Sādhanā is intended to support fitness and general wellness, not replace qualified medical professionals.

* Recommendations should account for declared limitations and relevant health considerations.
* AI-generated plans should be validated against backend safety rules.
* Pain or concerning symptoms should trigger appropriate precautions rather than unsupported diagnoses.
* User information should be handled with appropriate privacy and security safeguards.
